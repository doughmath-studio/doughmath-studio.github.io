QR Table Menu Maker

Type your menu, get a QR code, stick it on your tables. For cafes, bakeries and food trucks. Free, no sign-up.

The menu lives inside the link (or in your own Google Sheet). Nothing is uploaded to us.

In link mode your menu sits in the part of the link after the # sign. Browsers never send that part to any server. Your draft is saved only in this browser, and the guest menu page sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

4Get your QR code

The whole menu is packed into the link. Nothing to set up. To change a price you make a new QR code.

The QR never changes. You edit prices in your own Google Sheet, and guests see the change when they open or reload the menu.

  1. Use our ready-made Google Sheets template (with dropdowns, checks and an allergen list), or any Google Sheet laid out like the format below.
  2. In Google Sheets tap Share → General access → Anyone with the link → Viewer → Copy link.
  3. Paste it here.
For more privacy: File → Share → Publish to web → this tab → CSV, and paste that link instead

In Google Sheets choose File → Share → Publish to web. Pick the menu tab (not “Entire document”), choose Comma-separated values (.csv) and press Publish. Copy the link Google shows you and paste it in the box below. Only that tab is public, not the rest of the spreadsheet.

Build your own sheet

You do not need the template. Any Google Sheet works if the tab named Publish this tab has this layout (columns A to G):

#settings,name,currency,language,theme,footer
,Example Café & Bakery,zł,en,espresso,Wi-Fi: ExampleGuest · Instagram @examplecafe

section,item,price,description,allergens,tags,sold_out
Pastries,Almond croissant,13,Filled with almond cream,"1, 3, 7, 8",V,yes
Breakfast,Shakshuka,34,"Eggs in spiced tomato sauce, sourdough","1, 3","V, SPICY",
  • Row 1 is the word #settings followed by name, currency, language, theme, footer. Row 2 holds your values under them, and cell A2 stays empty. Row 3 is blank (the menu still works if it is missing).
  • Row 4 is the header: section, item, price, description, allergens, tags, sold_out. From row 5 on, one dish per row. Rows without a dish name are skipped. Sections show in the order they first appear. A blank section cell continues the section above.
  • currency: a code (PLN, EUR, USD) or a sign (zł, €, $). language: en, es, pt, fr, de, pl, uk or ru. theme: espresso, pistachio, berry or mono. Anything else falls back to en and espresso.
  • price: a number with a comma or a dot (12,50 or 12.5). A currency sign is fine. Text such as ask is shown as it is.
  • allergens: EU allergen numbers 1 to 14, separated by commas, semicolons or spaces. 1 gluten, 2 crustaceans, 3 eggs, 4 fish, 5 peanuts, 6 soy, 7 milk, 8 tree nuts, 9 celery, 10 mustard, 11 sesame, 12 sulphites, 13 lupin, 14 molluscs. Format this column as Plain text (Format → Number → Plain text) so Sheets does not turn "1, 7" into a number.
  • tags: V, VG, GF, SPICY, separated by commas, in any letter case (for example VG, GF). sold_out: yes greys the dish out as sold out. Leave it empty otherwise.

Payments (optional)

To add “Pay the bill”, put these two rows right under the settings values, then keep one blank row, then the header row that starts with section. Columns are found by their names, so the order does not matter.

#pay,enabled,tips,terminal,link,blik,note,review_link,feedback_whatsapp,feedback_email
,yes,"10, 15, 20",yes,https://paypal.me/yourcafe,+48 600 000 000,Ask staff for your receipt,https://g.page/r/your-cafe/review,+48 600 111 222,manager@yourcafe.com
  • enabled: yes or no. tips: up to 4 whole numbers from 1 to 50, such as 10, 15, 20. An empty cell means no tip buttons (guests can still type a tip). Leave the whole tips column out to get 10, 15, 20.
  • terminal: yes if you have a card terminal. The guest gets a big card with the total to show your waiter.
  • link: your own payment page. It has to start with https:// and be at most 300 characters. For a plain paypal.me/yourname link the total is added for the guest. blik: your BLIK phone number (only +, digits and spaces, 9 to 15 digits). note: plain text, up to 200 characters.
  • review_link: your Google review page (https only, up to 300 characters). feedback_whatsapp: the manager's WhatsApp number in international format (only +, digits and spaces, 9 to 15 digits). feedback_email: the manager's e-mail. All three are optional. After paying, guests can rate the visit and then see the same buttons whatever the rating: describe the visit on Google, and message the manager by WhatsApp and/or e-mail. If none of the three is filled in, the rating block is not shown.
  • Leave a cell empty to skip that way of paying. If nothing valid is left, guests see the menu only, as before.

Bill feed tab (optional)

Your till or your waiters can write the open bills into a second tab named Bill feed, one row per ordered item. A guest who scans a numbered card (step 5) sees the items of their table, can pay all of it, only their own items, or split it between people:

table,item,qty,price,waiter
12,Żurek,1,24.00,Marek
12,Pierogi ruskie,2,30.00,Marek
  • table: a whole number from 1 to 999. item: the name. qty: a whole number, empty means 1. price: the price of ONE item, with a comma or a dot (24,00 or 24.00). The line is qty × price. waiter: optional, see below. Extra or empty columns are fine. Rows without a table number, an item or a valid price are skipped.
  • The page reads this tab when the guest opens the Pay screen, or taps Refresh there. A change shows up a few seconds after you type it. The tab is read as it is: remove or empty the rows when a table pays.
  • Waiter names appear only if you type them in the sheet, and only with the waiter's consent. The waiter of a table is the last non-empty name in its rows. With an empty waiter column the guest sees no name anywhere.
  • Anyone who has the sheet link can read this tab. Put only table numbers, items, prices and (with consent) a first name in it, never guest names or contact details. Link mode and published sheets have no bill tab: guests type the amount.
  • The older tab named Bills (columns table, amount) still works when there is no Bill feed tab: guests then see the amount and can change it to split the bill.

NFC tag (optional)

A guest can also tap their phone on a small NFC tag instead of scanning the QR code. Write the link below to the tag with a free app such as NFC Tools. Use NTAG213 or NTAG215 stickers. iPhone XS and newer and Android phones with NFC switched on open the link without an app. Write the same link as the QR. Lock the tag after writing so nobody can change it.

5Print at home or in the office

Every card gets its own QR code with the table number inside, so the Pay screen knows the table. Stick each card on its own table.

Pages are A4. In the print dialog choose A4, margins None, scale 100% and turn on background graphics. On an iPhone or iPad choose A4 in the print options. US Letter paper: choose "Fit to page", things shrink about 6%.

Fake QR stickers happen. Someone can stick their own code over yours. Every card shows the web address under the QR, and your menu page shows your cafe name large at the top, so guests can check both. Use tamper-evident or doming stickers that cannot be peeled off cleanly, and look at your stickers every day or two.

6Sticker file for a print shop

For durable stickers with a clear protective coat. The file has 3 mm bleed, a 3 mm safe zone, black dots on white at error correction H, dots at least 0.5 mm wide and a 4-dot quiet zone.

Preview only. Red dashes: page edge with bleed. Magenta: die-cut line. Blue dashes: 3 mm safe zone. The guides are not in the print file.

The PDF has one page per table (the numbering is set in step 5). The preview and the SVG show this table.

PDF: one sticker per page, page size = sticker + 3 mm bleed on every side. Save it from a computer with Chrome, Edge or Firefox (Print → Save as PDF, margins None, background graphics on). SVG: the artwork plus a separate layer named CutContour (100% magenta line, 0.25 pt, no fill) for the die-cut. Text in the SVG is live text, so send the PDF too or ask the shop to outline fonts.

Ordering stickers: a short guide
  • Material: white polymer vinyl (or polyester) with a permanent adhesive.
  • Finish: epoxy doming (a 3D clear dome) or a gloss or matte lamination. Matte glares less, so phones scan it more easily.
  • Optional: tamper-evident "VOID" material, which shows a pattern if someone peels the sticker off.
  • Send: the PDF from this page. Add the SVG if the shop asks for a cut line.
  • Always ask for a proof before the full run. Scan the proof with two or three different phones, in bright light and in a dim room.
  • After you stick them: check your stickers regularly. A fake sticker can be stuck over a real one.
