Privacy

Last updated: 22 October 2026.

This website

  • We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.
  • The calculators run entirely in your browser. The numbers you type are not sent anywhere.
  • The Bento app also runs in your browser. It saves your inputs and recipes in your browser's local storage on your device, and keeps a copy of the app so it works offline. Nothing you type is sent to us. Clearing your browser data, or removing Bento from your Home Screen, deletes it.
  • Voice input in Bento is optional. When you tap the microphone, your browser's speech service (from Apple or Google, depending on your device) turns your speech into text. Bento does not record or store audio. Its answers are worked out on your device, and the conversation stays on your device.
  • The QR table menu maker (/qr/) and the guest menu page (/m/) set no cookies and use no analytics or third-party scripts. In link mode the whole menu is stored in the part of the link after the # sign. Browsers never send that part to any server, so we never receive it. The menu maker saves a draft only in your browser's local storage on your device; the "Delete my draft" button removes it. In Live mode the guest page reads the menu straight from the cafe owner's own Google Sheet (docs.google.com), which the owner has shared by link or published, with no cookies and no referrer sent. Google sees that request like any other web request. If the cafe owner switches on "Pay the bill", the guest page only shows amounts and sends the guest to the cafe's own way of paying (a payment link, a card terminal at the table or a BLIK phone number). No money passes through us, the page never asks for card details, and it never stores an amount or anything else: no cookies, no local storage, no analytics. The open bill of a table (the items, their prices and, only if the owner types it in the sheet and the waiter agrees, the waiter's first name) can be read from the owner's own Google Sheet in the same way as the menu. After paying, a guest can give a star rating and write a comment. Unless the cafe has switched on staff notifications (next point), the rating and the comment are not sent to us and are not stored anywhere: the page only builds links that open the cafe's own Google review page, WhatsApp or e-mail app, where the guest sends the message from their own app. The same buttons are shown for every rating.
  • Staff notifications (only if the cafe owner switches them on in the sheet). When a guest taps a button such as “Ask for the card terminal”, “I have sent the BLIK transfer”, the cafe’s payment link or “Send the rating to the café”, the guest page sends the table number, the amounts (bill, the guest’s share, tip), the way of paying, the waiter’s first name from the sheet and, for a rating, the stars and the comment to our notification service (fastpay-api.doughmath.workers.dev, run on Cloudflare, database in Europe). The service passes this to the cafe’s waiters and managers through Telegram bots and deletes it after 30 days. Nothing is sent before the guest taps. It never receives card details, sets no cookies and does not store IP addresses (it keeps only a salted hash that changes every day, to limit abuse, and deletes it the next day). After a guest asks for the terminal, the page asks the service every 15 seconds, for up to 15 minutes and only while the page is open, whether the waiter has marked the table as paid. Waiters and managers join the bots only through an invite and their own agreement; the bots keep their first name from the invite and their Telegram ID, and /stop deletes both.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record technical data such as IP addresses to run and protect the service, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Links to Etsy and Pinterest take you to their sites, where their own privacy policies apply.

Email updates

If you sign up for email updates, we collect only your email address, through a Google Form. We use it to send occasional emails with new free tools and pricing tips, at most once a month. The legal basis is your consent. You can unsubscribe at any time by replying "unsubscribe" or by writing to us, and we will delete your address. We never sell or share your address.
