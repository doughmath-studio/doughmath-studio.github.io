About DoughMath Studio

Pricing and costing tools for home bakers, cafes, food trucks and caterers.

I'm Ivan, a professional cook. In a restaurant kitchen, every dish is costed before it reaches the menu: what the ingredients cost after trimming, what goes on the plate, what the plate must sell for. Many home bakers and small food businesses never get that step, and price by guesswork.

DoughMath Studio puts the kitchen method into simple tools. The calculators on this site are free and need no sign-up. For people who want to cost every recipe, track orders and see monthly profit, there are full spreadsheet templates for Google Sheets and Excel in our Etsy shop.

How the tools are made

The templates and calculators are built with the help of AI tools. Every formula and example is tested before release, and sample numbers are checked by an independent recalculation.
