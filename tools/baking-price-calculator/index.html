How the price is worked out

The calculator spreads every batch cost over the pieces you can actually sell, then adds your margin on top:

Price = true cost per piece ÷ (1 − margin)

With a 35% margin, a cookie that costs you 1.45 sells for 1.45 ÷ 0.65 = 2.23. Margin is not the same as markup: a 35% markup on the same cookie would give only 1.96, and your real margin would be 26%.

The step most people skip is their own time. If your hours are not in the cost, a price that looks profitable can still mean working for free. Read the full method in How to price baked goods: a 6-step formula.
