Why the tax matters

VAT or sales tax is money you collect for the government, not revenue. Food cost % must be measured against what you keep:

Food cost % = plate cost ÷ menu price without tax

With 23% VAT, a 12.30 dish is really 10.00 to you. A 3.00 plate looks like 24% food cost, but the real figure is 30%. That gap is the difference between "on target" and "where did the money go".
